## C. Implementation Requirements for All Authorized Reclaimed Water Uses — 84.10(A)

### C.1 — Compliance with 84.9(A)(4)(a), (b), (d)

> Reclaimed (recycled) water will be used to irrigate the landscape at the {PROJECT_NAME} site. The landscape at this site is accessible to the public, but irrigation typically takes place outside of normal use times (nighttime/early morning). Where food prep areas or water fixtures are present, irrigation has been designed to avoid these areas. The irrigation system is designed to irrigate only the intended landscape areas and to avoid spraying water on buildings, domestic drinking water facilities, loading docks, or other areas where human contact with reclaimed water is possible. Potable water is also used at this site for domestic purposes; the recycled water irrigation system is completely separate from the potable water supply. No other non-potable water sources are used at this site.

### C.2 — Compliance with 84.10(A)(1)

> There is no commingling of multiple water sources at this site. The recycled water irrigation system is served by a dedicated {TAP_SIZE}-inch recycled water meter from Denver Water's recycled water system and is completely separate from the potable water supply. No supplementing of reclaimed water with potable or other non-potable water sources occurs at this site. Backflow prevention is provided at the point of connection in accordance with Denver Water requirements.

### C.3 — Compliance with 84.10(A)(2)

> All recycled water piping, conveyances, and appurtenances are colored purple or installed with purple identification. The recycled water service line is Type K copper, colored purple, with "CAUTION: RECYCLED WATER-DO NOT DRINK" marking. Sprinkler laterals are CL200 PVC, colored purple, with "CAUTION: RECYCLED WATER-DO NOT DRINK" marking. The mainline is SCH40 PVC, colored purple, with "CAUTION: RECYCLED WATER-DO NOT DRINK" marking. Drip tubing is 0.75-inch UV-resistant purple stripe PE. All above-ground components including valve boxes, controller pedestals, and quick coupler assemblies are purple or marked with purple identification. All pipe, fittings, and appurtenances are marked or stamped with the words "NONPOTABLE RECLAIMED WATER – NOT FOR DRINKING or DO NOT DRINK" or equivalent "CAUTION: RECYCLED WATER-DO NOT DRINK."

### C.6 — Compliance with 84.10(A)(5)

> The irrigation system and piping feeding it are completely separate from the domestic potable water supply and no buildings will be supplied with recycled water. The irrigation system is designed to irrigate only the intended landscape areas and to avoid spraying water on buildings, loading docks, domestic drinking water facilities, facilities where food is being prepared, or other areas where human contact with reclaimed water is possible. The system uses {SPRINKLER_TYPES} with matched precipitation rates and proper arc adjustments to confine spray to landscaped areas. Drip irrigation is used for all trees and shrubs, eliminating overspray in those areas. Qualified, trained personnel (or a professional landscaping company) will control all irrigation system operations and equipment will be inspected and tested on a regular basis and adjusted and repaired when necessary to ensure proper operation. Irrigation will be timed to take place during low-use times of day (nighttime/early morning) and in high-use areas, irrigation will cease with enough time for drying prior to typical use.

### C.7 — Compliance with 84.10(A)(6)

> The irrigation system includes a master valve and flow sensor at each point of connection that will detect abnormal flow conditions and automatically shut down the system in the event of a leak or broken sprinkler head. The system is monitored by a {CONTROLLER_TYPE} controller with flow monitoring capability. Leaks in plumbing, fixtures, equipment, sprinkler systems, or any other mechanism where reclaimed water is used or transmitted will be repaired immediately upon discovery. If the leak cannot be repaired immediately, leak repair will be scheduled, and reclaimed water use will be discontinued until repairs can be made. The irrigation controller and flow sensor will isolate the zone where the leak is detected. Spills will be reported in accordance with §25-8-601(2) CRS — notification to Denver Water and CDPHE will be made in writing within 30 days. If water leaving the site exceeds 10,000 gallons, the spill will be reported immediately (within 24 hours) to the CDPHE Spill Hotline at 877-518-5608, with a written report within 5 days.

Disposal of reclaimed water is through approved irrigation use only; water is applied on landscape at agronomic rates in accordance with the site's agronomic rate analysis. ## D. Implementation Requirements for Landscape Irrigation Uses — 84.10(B)(6), (B)(7), (B)(8)

*This site is Unrestricted-Access Landscape Irrigation (publicly accessible landscape). The following responses address the requirements for 84.10(B)(7).*

### D.1 — Compliance with agronomic rate for Unrestricted-Access Landscape Irrigation — 84.10(B)(7)(a)

> The irrigation system is designed to meet the water needs of vegetation while minimizing ponding, runoff, overspray, and nutrient loading. The system incorporates the following measures:
>
> - **Rain sensors** (Hunter wireless Mini-Click sensors) are installed at each controller location to interrupt irrigation during and after precipitation events
> - **Smart controllers** (Hunter ACC2) provide ET-based scheduling to match plant water requirements and reduce usage during and after precipitation
> - **Drip irrigation** is used for all trees and shrubs (Rain Bird XFS-CV-06-12 drip line with 0.6 GPH emitters at 12-inch spacing), delivering water directly to the root zone and minimizing evaporation and overspray
> - **Matched precipitation rate sprinkler heads** (Hunter MP Rotators and Pro Sprays) are used for turf and native grass areas at 70% system efficiency
> - **Separate irrigation zones** for turf (bluegrass), native grass, and trees/shrubs allow different application rates matched to plant water requirements (Kc: bluegrass = 0.88, native grass = 0.3, trees/shrubs = 0.5)
> - **Flow sensors and master valves** at each point of connection detect abnormal flow and automatically shut down the system to prevent runoff from leaks or breaks
> - The site water budget demonstrates total annual application of {GALLONS} gallons across {TOTAL_ACRES} irrigated acres, which corresponds to {LOADING} lbs/acre of nitrogen loading — well below the agronomic need of {NEED_PER_ACRE} lbs/acre (see attached Agronomic Rate Analysis)
>
> Qualified, trained personnel will operate and maintain the irrigation system equipment, which will be inspected and tested on a regular basis and adjusted and repaired when necessary to ensure operation in accordance with design. Irrigation will be timed to take place during low-use times of day.

### D.2 — Compliance with windblown spray and human exposure for Unrestricted-Access Landscape Irrigation — 84.10(B)(7)(c)

> The irrigation system and piping feeding it are completely separate from the domestic potable water supply and no buildings will be supplied with recycled water. The irrigation system is designed to irrigate only the intended landscape areas and to avoid spraying water on buildings, loading docks, domestic drinking water facilities, facilities where food is being prepared, or other areas where human contact with reclaimed water is possible. The system uses Hunter MP Rotators with low application rates and Pro Spray heads with matched precipitation nozzles, properly adjusted for arc and radius to confine spray within landscape areas. Drip irrigation is used for all trees and shrubs, eliminating windblown spray in those areas. Qualified, trained personnel (or a professional landscaping company) will control all irrigation system operations and equipment will be inspected and tested on a regular basis and adjusted and repaired when necessary to ensure proper operation. Irrigation will be timed to take place during low-use times of day (nighttime/early morning), and in high-use areas, irrigation will cease with enough time for drying prior to typical use.
