# UASMP Form Section Guide for TIE

Section-by-section guide to the Regulation 84 UASMP (User Application and Site Management Plan) Landscape Irrigation form. Focuses on what TIE (as the irrigation designer) needs to provide vs. what the User (property owner) and Treater (Denver Water) handle.

## Form Identification

- **Form title**: "REGULATION 84 USER APPLICATION AND SITE MANAGEMENT PLAN (UASMP) — Landscape Irrigation"
- **CDPHE form number**: WQC2017-2 (Rev Jan 2024)
- **Download**: https://oitco.hylandcloud.com/CDPHERMPop/docpop/docpop.aspx?clienttype=html&docid=5080744
- **Submit via**: CDPHE reuse application submission form (online portal)

## Part I — Completed by the User (with TIE providing technical input)

### Section C: Implementation Requirements for All Authorized Reclaimed Water Uses

These are narrative descriptions. The Denver Water cheat sheet provides example language. TIE should draft these based on the project's irrigation design.

**C.1 (§84.9(A)(4))** — How reclaimed water is used, public contact, water sources
> Example: "Reclaimed (recycled) water will be used to irrigate the landscape at the described site. The landscape at this site is accessible to the public, but irrigation typically takes place outside of normal use times. Where food prep areas or water fixtures are present, irrigation has been designed to avoid these areas. Potable water is also used at this site for domestic purposes."

**C.2 (§84.10(A)(1))** — Cross connection / supplementing with potable
> If no commingling (typical): "There is no commingling of recycled water with potable or other water sources at this site. The irrigation system is served exclusively by the recycled water system."

**C.3 (§84.10(A)(2))** — Purple pipe and labeling
> Example: "All visible pipes, appurtenances and fixtures are denoted as recycled water equipment with purple color and/or tags, wraps, stamping or other identification methods that include the language 'NONPOTABLE RECLAIMED WATER – NOT FOR DRINKING or DO NOT DRINK'. The irrigation plans specify purple-colored Type K copper service line, purple CL200 PVC sprinkler lateral, and purple-striped PE drip tubing with 'CAUTION: RECYCLED WATER-DO NOT DRINK' labeling."

**C.4 (§84.10(A)(3))** — Education and training
> List dates of past/upcoming Denver Water annual training sessions. User fills this out.

**C.5 (§84.10(A)(4))** — Signage
> Example: "Signage is placed throughout the site indicating that non-potable recycled water is being used. All signs include a graphic indicating the water is not for drinking and include the phrases 'Recycled Water' and 'Do Not Drink.' Signs are posted at all entrances and at intervals along the perimeter of the irrigated areas."

**C.6 (§84.10(A)(5))** — Confining irrigation to authorized areas
> Example: "The irrigation system and piping feeding it are completely separate from the domestic potable water supply and no buildings will be supplied with recycled water. The irrigation system is designed to irrigate only the intended landscape areas and to avoid spraying water on buildings, domestic drinking water facilities, facilities where food is being prepared or other areas where human contact with reclaimed water is possible. Qualified, trained personnel will control all irrigation system operations and equipment will be inspected and tested on a regular basis and adjusted and repaired when necessary. Irrigation will be timed to take place during low use times of day and in high-use areas, irrigation will cease with enough time for drying prior to typical use."

**C.7 (§84.10(A)(6))** — Leak repair
> Example: "Leaks in the irrigation system will be repaired immediately upon discovery. If a leak cannot be repaired immediately, irrigation to the affected area will be discontinued until repairs can be made. Spills exceeding 10,000 gallons will be reported immediately to the CDPHE Spill Hotline at 877-518-5608."

**C.8 (§84.10(A)(7))** — Disposal
> "Reclaimed water is only applied to landscape at agronomic rates. No disposal of reclaimed water occurs outside of approved irrigation use."

**C.9 (§84.10(A)(8))** — Check YES (operation by authorized personnel only)

**C.10 (§84.10(A)(9))** — Vehicle/tank hauling — usually "Not applicable" for landscape irrigation

**C.11 (§84.10(A)(10))** — Check YES (coordinated with Treater, has copy of Reg 84)

**C.12 (§84.10(A)(11))** — Check YES (UASMP kept on site). Describe where.

**C.13 (§84.10(A)(12))** — Check YES (will report violations)

**C.14 (§84.10(A)(13))** — Check YES (will furnish info to Division)

**C.15 (§84.10(A)(14))** — Impoundments — usually "No" for typical landscape irrigation

### Section D: Implementation Requirements for Landscape Irrigation

**D.1 (§84.10(B)(7)(a))** — Agronomic rate compliance
> Example: "The irrigation system is designed to meet the water needs of vegetation while minimizing ponding, runoff, overspray and nutrient loading. The system includes rain sensors (Hunter wireless Mini-Click) and smart controllers (Hunter ACC2) to reduce usage during and after precipitation events. The system is designed with separate irrigation zones for different vegetation types (turf, native grass, trees/shrubs) with appropriate application rates for each. Qualified, trained personnel will operate and maintain the irrigation system, which will be inspected and tested on a regular basis and adjusted and repaired when necessary. An agronomic rate analysis is attached demonstrating that nitrogen loading is below the agronomic need of the vegetation. See Attachment E.3."

**D.2 (§84.10(B)(7)(c))** — Windblown spray
> Similar to C.6 — restate how the system avoids spray outside landscaped areas, mention timing of irrigation relative to use periods.

**D.3** — Check YES (flood and sheet irrigation prohibited)

**D.4 (§84.10(B)(7)(d))** — Signage (same as C.5, restate)

**D.5** — Check YES (no reclaimed water piping to residential structures)

**D.6** — SKIP (Category 1 restricted access only — does not apply to Denver Water Category 3)

**D.7** — SKIP (resident-controlled only)

**D.8** — SKIP (resident-controlled only)

### Section E: Required Attachments

**E.1 (§84.9(A)(5))** — Check "Attached": Site map showing areas where reclaimed water will be used
**E.2 (§84.9(A)(5)(b))** — Check "Attached": Map showing acreage and type of landscape being irrigated
**E.3 (§84.9(A)(6))** — Check "Attached": Agronomic rate analysis (see `agronomic-rate-analysis.md` reference)

### Section F: Localized Treatment Systems

**SKIP entirely** — Denver Water is a centralized system. This section only applies to localized (on-site) treatment systems.

### Section G: Certification Signature

**User signs here** — TIE does NOT sign. The Legally Responsible Individual must be:
- Corporation: principal executive officer (VP level or above)
- Partnership: general partner
- Sole proprietorship: the proprietor
- Municipal/state/public: principal executive officer or ranking elected official

## Part II — Completed by Denver Water (Treater)

Denver Water fills out:
- Treater Authorization Number (COE012000)
- Legally Responsible Individual (Denver Water's signatory)
- Facility Information (Denver Water's recycling facility)
- Treater Certification Signature

## Denver Water Cheat Sheet Key Points

- Treater Authorization Number: **COE012000**
- Reason for Application: "NEW USER AUTHORIZATION"
- Certified Operator (A.2): Mark "Not Applicable"
- Water type (B): Centralized Category 3
- Section F: Ignore (localized systems only)
- Sections D.6-D.8: Ignore (Category 1 / resident-controlled only)
- Part II: Denver Water fills out after reviewing Part I
